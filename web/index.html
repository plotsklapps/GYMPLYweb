GYMPLY – Minimalist Offline Workout Tracker

The open-source, 100% free, ad-free offline workout tracker for Android.

Core App Highlights

GYMPLY is designed for fitness enthusiasts who value simplicity, complete privacy, and zero bloat. No accounts, no paywalls, no internet connection required, and 0% ads.

  • 100% Private – Your workout data stays locally on your device. No cloud storage, no data collection.
  • 4,000+ Exercises – Comprehensive database with clear visual exercise guides.
  • Powerful Insights – Detailed workout history, volume tracking, and personal records.
  • Offline-First – Train anywhere without needing an internet connection.
  • Nostr Integration – Optional private workout sharing via the Nostr protocol.
