GYMPLY – Minimalist Offline Workout Tracker

The open-source, 100% free, ad-free offline workout tracker for Android and Web.

Core Value Pillars

GYMPLY is designed for fitness enthusiasts who value simplicity, complete privacy, and zero bloat. No accounts, no paywalls, no internet connection required, and 0% ads.

  • 100% Private – Your workout data stays locally on your device. No cloud storage, no tracking, no data collection.
  • 4,000+ Exercises Database – Comprehensive database with clear visual exercise guides, categories, and custom movement tracking.
  • Powerful Training Insights – Detailed workout history, volume tracking, sets/reps logging, and personal records.
  • Offline-First Architecture – Train anywhere, anytime without needing an active internet connection.
  • Nostr Protocol Integration – Optional decentralized, private workout sharing via the Nostr protocol.
